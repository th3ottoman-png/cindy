## Two defects found by the live run, both fixed red-first

1. **Double counting.** An orphan home was planned as a whole *and* its catalog/uv/logs were listed
   again, inflating `estimatedFreedBytes`. Red test: `view.preview.filter(d => d.path.startsWith(home + sep))`
   returned 3 entries. Fix: the host stops emitting interior candidates once the whole home is a
   candidate. Green: the interior list is empty and `estimatedFreedBytes === whole.sizeBytes`.
2. **Retention could never fire.** The first live scan reported `oldest today` and 0 reclaimable
   items at retention 7. Independent measurement showed **all 169 homes < 1 day idle** while their
   transcripts were 9–16 days old. Cause: opening `state.db` refreshes **`state.db-shm`** (0.87 h
   old in a home whose every other entry was 231 h old), and the idle notion inherited from
   `reapOrphanHomes` counts it. Fix: `homeIdleMs` ignores `-shm` and `.lock` entries (`-wal` is
   kept — it only moves on a real write). Red test: a home aged 40 days with a fresh `state.db-shm`
   was not planned; after the fix its catalog is. Live after the fix: `oldest 15 days ago`,
   110 items / 666.5 MB.

## Mutation checks (planner)

- Removing the `state.db` guard → **1 test fails** (`never prunes the transcript…`).
- Removing the newest-backup rule → **4 tests fail**.
- Restored → 34 pass.

## Final state

- Retention restored to the default **30** through the real input + Save button; the section then
  reads `0 items · about 0 B`, which is correct: the oldest home is 15 days old, so nothing has
  aged past 30 days yet. `housekeeping.json` verified on disk:
  `{ "retentionDays": 30, "pruneAtStartup": false }`.
- **Nothing was deleted.** No home, cache, backup or log was removed by this row.
- The scan cap was lowered from 8 s to **4 s** after measuring 1.1 s, because the walk is synchronous
  on the main process; a truncated scan is reported as a lower bound with a Remeasure button.
- Dev app stopped after the proof; ports free.

## Not in this row (13.2c)

Prune with preview + confirm, the freed-bytes result, the optional startup run, and the
`protectedSessionIds` wiring for **open / scheduled / pinned** conversations. The host already
protects homes whose daemon record is `running: true`; because nothing deletes in 13.2b that is
sufficient here, and `register.ts` carries a comment stating 13.2c must pass the full protected set
before it removes anything. Tier 2 (soft-deleted conversations' homes) also needs the
Cindy `sessions.status` join, which is 13.2c work.
